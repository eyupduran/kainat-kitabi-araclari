Kainat Kitabi Araclari

A private publishing tool for a single YouTube channel.

Kainat Kitabi Araclari is a command-line tool that runs only on the channel owner's own computer. The channel owner makes short animated educational videos in Turkish and uses this tool to publish them to their own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. The tool is not offered to the public, has no other users, and has no website other than these information pages.

What the tool does

  • Uploads the owner's finished videos to the owner's channel, with title, description, tags, category and a scheduled publish time.
  • Sets the custom thumbnail and uploads the owner's own Turkish subtitle file.
  • Adds each video to the owner's playlist for its series.
  • Posts the owner's first comment under the owner's own video.
  • Reads YouTube Analytics reports for the owner's own channel, so the owner can see how the videos perform.
  • Keeps the owner's own channel tidy: corrects the owner's subtitles, orders the owner's playlists and sets their cover images, and arranges the sections of the owner's channel page by series.

It does not read, collect or process data about viewers or any other YouTube user, and it does not access any channel except the owner's.

Google permissions it asks for

  • youtube.upload: upload videos and thumbnails.
  • youtube.force-ssl: manage playlists, subtitles and the owner's comment.
  • yt-analytics.readonly: read analytics for the owner's own channel.
